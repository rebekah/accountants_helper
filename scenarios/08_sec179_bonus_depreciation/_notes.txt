SCENARIO 08 — SECTION 179 / BONUS DEPRECIATION TIMING DIFFERENCE
=================================================================
The company purchased $50,000 of equipment in 2023 and elected full
Section 179 expensing on the tax return. On the books, only $10,000 of
straight-line depreciation was recorded (5-year life). This created a
$40,000 timing difference between book and tax income.

Key numbers:
  2023 book depreciation:          $10,000
  2023 tax depreciation (Sec 179): $50,000
  Extra depreciation on tax:       $40,000

  2023 book net income:            $50,000
  2023 tax net income:             $10,000
  2023 book ending RE:            $250,000
  2023 Schedule L ending RE:      $210,000  ← $40,000 lower

  2024 GL opening RE (wrong):     $250,000  (carried forward book basis)
  2024 GL opening RE (correct):   $210,000  (prior Schedule L basis)

Expected adjustment:
  Dr. Retained Earnings                    $40,000
  Cr. Prior Year Adjustment (clearing)     $40,000
