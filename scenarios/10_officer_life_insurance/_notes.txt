SCENARIO 10 — NON-DEDUCTIBLE OFFICER LIFE INSURANCE (IRC §264)
==============================================================
The company paid $15,000 in officer life insurance premiums in 2023.
Under IRC §264, premiums on company-owned life insurance where the
company is the beneficiary are not deductible. The full $15,000 was
expensed on the books but added back on the tax return, making the
Schedule L ending RE $15,000 higher than the GL.

Key numbers:
  2023 officer life insurance expense:     $15,000
  Deductible on tax return:                    $0
  Add-back to taxable income:              $15,000

  2023 book net income:                    $20,000
  2023 tax net income:                     $35,000
  2023 book ending RE:                    $300,000
  2023 Schedule L ending RE:              $315,000  ← $15,000 higher

  2024 GL opening RE (wrong):             $300,000  (carried forward book basis)
  2024 GL opening RE (correct):           $315,000  (prior Schedule L basis)

Expected adjustment:
  Dr. Prior Year Adjustment (clearing)     $15,000
  Cr. Retained Earnings                    $15,000
