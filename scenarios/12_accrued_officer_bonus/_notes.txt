SCENARIO 12 — ACCRUED OFFICER BONUS TIMING (IRC §267)
======================================================
The company accrued a $25,000 year-end bonus to the majority
shareholder/officer. Under IRC §267, compensation owed to a related
party is only deductible in the year it is actually paid. The bonus
was not paid until February 2024, so it was not deductible on the
2023 return. The $25,000 add-back made taxable income — and therefore
Schedule L RE — $25,000 higher than the GL.

Key numbers:
  2023 accrued officer bonus (books):      $25,000
  Deductible on 2023 tax return:               $0  (not paid until 2024)
  Add-back to taxable income:              $25,000

  2023 book net income:                    $35,000
  2023 tax net income:                     $60,000
  2023 book ending RE:                    $115,000
  2023 Schedule L ending RE:              $140,000  ← $25,000 higher

  2024 GL opening RE (wrong):             $115,000  (carried forward book basis)
  2024 GL opening RE (correct):           $140,000  (prior Schedule L basis)

Expected adjustment:
  Dr. Prior Year Adjustment (clearing)     $25,000
  Cr. Retained Earnings                    $25,000
