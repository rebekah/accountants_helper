SCENARIO 09 — MEALS & ENTERTAINMENT ADD-BACK (IRC §274)
========================================================
The company deducted $10,000 of meals & entertainment on the books.
Under IRC §274, only 50% ($5,000) is deductible on the tax return.
The $5,000 add-back increased taxable income above book income, making
the Schedule L ending RE $5,000 higher than the GL.

Key numbers:
  2023 M&E on books:               $10,000
  2023 M&E deductible on tax:       $5,000
  Non-deductible add-back:          $5,000

  2023 book net income:             $45,000
  2023 tax net income:              $50,000
  2023 book ending RE:             $195,000
  2023 Schedule L ending RE:       $200,000  ← $5,000 higher

  2024 GL opening RE (wrong):      $195,000  (carried forward book basis)
  2024 GL opening RE (correct):    $200,000  (prior Schedule L basis)

Expected adjustment:
  Dr. Prior Year Adjustment (clearing)      $5,000
  Cr. Retained Earnings                     $5,000
