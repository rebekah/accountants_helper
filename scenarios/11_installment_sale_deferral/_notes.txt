SCENARIO 11 — INSTALLMENT SALE — GAIN DEFERRED FOR TAX
=======================================================
The company sold land in 2023 for a $30,000 gain. The full gain was
recognized on the books in 2023. For tax purposes, the installment
method was used (IRC §453): only one-third of the proceeds were
collected in 2023, so only one-third of the gain ($10,000) was
recognized on the return. The remaining $20,000 is deferred until
future installments are collected.

Key numbers:
  2023 book gain on sale:          $30,000
  2023 tax-recognized gain:        $10,000  (1/3 collected)
  Deferred gain:                   $20,000

  2023 book net income:            $50,000
  2023 tax net income:             $30,000
  2023 book ending RE:            $170,000
  2023 Schedule L ending RE:      $150,000  ← $20,000 lower

  2024 GL opening RE (wrong):     $170,000  (carried forward book basis)
  2024 GL opening RE (correct):   $150,000  (prior Schedule L basis)

Expected adjustment:
  Dr. Retained Earnings                    $20,000
  Cr. Prior Year Adjustment (clearing)     $20,000
